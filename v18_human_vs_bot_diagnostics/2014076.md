### T14 — a Red re-touching blue's clued r2 collapses green's hole cards too early

- **Human:** the Red singles out blue's r2, already clued by the T12 2. That card is
  playable in the worlds where green played y1 then r1, and trash where green played
  r1 then r2. Green must not collapse until blue acts on the card: a discard means
  the r2 was played.
- **Bot:**
  - Before T14, green's hole cards are o11 `{r1,r2}` and o13 `{r1,y1}`, and its o15
    is called to play as `{y1,y2}`.
  - At T14 green narrowed o11 and o13 privately to the worlds that make the Red a
    play call, which gave its o15 `{y2}`.
  - At T16 blue discarded the r2.
  - `scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2014076 17`: green's own stacks read `120101`, the
    truth is `210101`, and the pair and common views disagree (`≠`).
- **Cause:** the outside-seat world narrowing
  (`src/conventions/tiiah/interpret_clue.cpp`, "An OUTSIDE seat also tries the
  worlds of its own hole cards", v17.1.0) narrows at once. It never asks whether the
  called card was already clued and dead in some of the worlds. The shared rule 2
  (`evidence_from`, `superposition.cpp`) took the call as evidence that the r2 was
  still needed in the same way, at every seat.
- **Resolution (v18.12.0):**
  - **Deferred collapse** (tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §1e): a COLOUR clue whose call
    re-touches an already-clued card, playable in only some worlds, collapses
    nothing. The outside-seat narrowing is skipped, and rule 2 takes no evidence
    from it while a hole card could still be that identity.
  - **Rule 7's shared form:** a named card thrown away keeps only the worlds of every
    seat's hole cards in which it was already trash, at every seat. Blue's T16 throw
    settles green's o11 as the r2.
  - Tests: `tests/test_tiiah/test_replay_2014076_deferred_collapse_on_a_retouched_card.cpp`
    (green's stacks at T18 are `210101`, o11 is settled, and the shared view has red
    on 2) and `tests/test_tiiah/test_deferred_collapse.cpp` (the re-touch waits, the
    discard resolves it, and a first touch collapses at once).
  - `scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2014076 17` after: every view agrees across seats (no
    `≠`), and green's private stacks are `210101`, the truth.
- **Status:** fixed in v18.12.0.
