## The human's analysis (verbatim)

> Turn 7: because TIIAH is considered a hard variant, we prefer to not lock players
> if their chop is worse than a 1-away-from-playable card (i.e. critical, playable,
> or 1 away from playable), especially if they have a standing play - in this case,
> y1 from yagami_green. This rule should be implemented in the decision making
> process so that yagami_green plays y1.
>
> Turn 10: This is a more egregious example that demands an investigation -
> yagami_green still has a standing play {y1} but is now giving a clue that does not
> even save a card in yagami_black's hand. The {y1} should always be played here
> given yagami_black's chop is a same-hand-dupe.

## Items

### T7 — green locks black over a y4 chop instead of playing its y1

- **Human:** in TIIAH, do not lock a player whose chop is worse than one away from
  playable, i.e. anything but a critical, playable or one-away card. That holds most
  of all when Alice has a standing play. Green should play the y1.
- **Bot:**
  - Green held o0, called to play as `{y1}`, at 7 tokens. It gave 2 to black, which
    touched o8 (an r2 on the lock slot) and read as a LOCK
    (`reactor0.choose_clue` rung `3.bob_chop`, shape `stable_lock`, tier HIGH via
    H1). That is rung 3.7.
  - Black's chop, the newest unclued card, was o17: a y4, with yellow on 0, so
    three away and not critical.
  - `scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2018365 7`: every view, and the truth, is `000010`.
- **Cause:** rung 3.7 (`rung_3`, `src/conventions/reactor0/decision.cpp`) locks
  Bob when three of his cards are close to playing, whatever his chop is. Green was
  occupied, so the tier gate wanted HIGH. H1 supplied it, since the y4 is endangered
  (`at_risk_chop`): no other copy is in sight.
- **Resolution (v20.2.0):** under Throw It in a Hole, rung 3.7 locks only when
  Bob's chop is critical, playable or one away from playable on Alice's own stacks
  (`chop_worth_a_lock`, `src/conventions/reactor0/state_eval.cpp:167-179`, read at
  `src/conventions/reactor0/decision.cpp:1801-1814`). 3.6b, which only replaces
  that lock, goes with it. See reactor0 `DECISION_MAKING.md` priority 3, item 7, and
  tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §2h.
  - Rerun, `logs/yagami_green-2018365.log --turn 7`: `clue rank value=2 → player 1`
    before, `play(order=0)` (the y1) after.
  - Tests:
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2018365_no_lock_over_far_chop.cpp`,
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_no_lock_over_far_chop.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed in v20.2.0.

### T10 — at 8 tokens green gives a clue that saves nothing instead of playing its y1

- **Human:** green still has the standing `{y1}`. Its clue saves nothing in black's
  hand, and black's chop is a same-hand dupe, so the y1 should be played.
- **Bot:**
  - Green held o0 `{y1}` at 8 tokens. It gave Blue to black, touching only the
    already-clued o6 b3 (`reactor0.choose_clue` rung
    `4.locked_eight_clues_or_low_pace`, shape `other`, tier 0).
  - Black's chop was the new o18, an r4. He also holds o9, the other r4.
- **Cause:** `priority_4_applies` (`src/conventions/reactor0/decision.cpp`) opens
  §4 at 8 tokens unconditionally, and §4's floor always returns a clue. Its
  premise is that at 8 tokens cluing is Alice's only alternative to burning a card.
  That is false when she holds a known play. The pace arm already asks whether
  Alice has a known play (4a); the 8-token arm did not. At 8 tokens an occupied
  Alice also skips the tier gate (rule 1b), so nothing else stopped the clue.
- **Resolution (v20.2.0):** at 8 tokens §4 now opens only on the pace arm's
  qualifiers: Alice has no known play (4a); Bob holds a hidden playable that a
  clue moves now while Cathy has none (4b); or a clue gets two plays (4c)
  (`priority_4_applies`, `src/conventions/reactor0/decision.cpp:2018-2029`). This
  applies to every convention that uses reactor0's ladder. See reactor0
  `DECISION_MAKING.md` priority 4, and tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §2i.
  - Rerun, `logs/yagami_green-2018365.log --turn 10`: `clue colour value=3 → player 1`
    before, `play(order=0)` after.
  - Test:
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2018365_play_over_forced_clue_at_eight.cpp`;
    the gate itself is pinned in
    `tests/test_reactor0/test_decision_making/test_section_four_gate.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed in v20.2.0.
