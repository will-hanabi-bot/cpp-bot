### T34 — will-bot67 plays a spent reaction instead of saving Bob's playable chop

- **Human:** will-bot69's chop is a playable b2 at risk, since black's chop is a
  trash m1. will-bot67's m3 can wait: a Blue gets the b2 played. And will-bot67's m3
  should not be an urgent reaction any more, because its target has already left the
  receiver's hand.
- **Bot:**
  - T30 (log): black's 3 to will-bot69 was a reactive, with will-bot67 reacting on o4
    `{ra3}` and will-bot69 receiving.
  - T31: will-bot67 deferred, cluing instead. T32: will-bot69 played o33, the paired
    target.
  - T34: o4 was still marked urgent (`CFU`), so Precedence step 2 played it
    (`precedence.urgent_reaction`). The clue phase, where §2e saves a stuck Bob's
    playable chop, never ran.
- **Cause:** the rule exists (reactor0 `DECISION_MAKING.md` Precedence step 2,
  v9.3.0). Rule 0 of the call invariants, `relegate_spent_reactions`, clears `urgent`
  once the reaction's target, `ConvData::react_target_order`, has left the receiver's
  hand. Only reactor0's walk records that field (`record_react_target`). Throw It in a
  Hole's own walk, `tiiah::interpret_reactive`, never did, so the field stayed -1 and
  rule 0 skipped every TIIAH reaction.
- **Resolution (v20.7.0):** the rule restored for Throw It in a Hole. The TIIAH walk
  now records the pairing (`react_target_order`) on the reacter's card wherever it
  makes the call (`src/conventions/tiiah/interpret_reactive.cpp:751-762`, and the
  ASCR pairing at `:492-496`). So rule 0 relegates a deferred reaction once the
  receiver has played its target. See reactor0 `DECISION_MAKING.md` Precedence step
  2 and tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §2j.
  - Rerun, `logs/will-bot67-2018759.log --turn 34`: `play(order=4)` before,
    `clue colour value=3 → player 1` (Blue to will-bot69, rung `3.bob_chop`,
    `stable_play` on the b2) after.
  - Tests:
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2018759_spent_reaction_not_urgent_saves_bobs_chop.cpp`,
