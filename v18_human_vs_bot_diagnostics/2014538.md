## Items

### T21 — green spends a clue on a duplicate n2 at 2 clues

- **Human:** acceptable, with no fix needed. But at 3 or fewer clues a human is far
  more conservative, especially with a player locked. At 2 or fewer with one locked,
  clues are for saving criticals or getting playables on chop played.
- **Bot:**
  - Green (2 tokens) gave Brown to black (`3.bob_chop`, `stable_play`). It called
    black's chop o1, an n2, while green's own o10 was the called n2 blue's T20 Brown
    named.
  - Blue was locked (every card chop-moved).
  - Black threw the o1 at T22.
- **Status:** guidance, recorded only. By ruling, no low-clue gate for now; revisit
  it when more diagnostics show where it matters.

### T23 — locked blue gives a stalling 3 instead of Green on the playable g3

- **Human:** blue can see the g3 is playable, and a colour clue to black is stable
  because green holds a called n2. A colour stable clue should outrank the rank 3.
- **Bot:**
  - Blue (2 tokens, locked) gave 3 to black (o0 y3, o4 p3, o26 g3), read as a STALL
    (`reactor0.choose_clue` rung `4.locked_eight_clues_or_low_pace`, shape
    `other`).
  - Green (blue's Bob) held the standing n2 call and black held none, so the reverse
    position held. By role inversion, Green to black was a stable play on o26.
- **Cause:** a locked Alice decides in section 4 (`rung_4`,
  `src/conventions/reactor0/decision.cpp`). Rung 4.1 takes `pool_stable_play`,
  which admits stable clues **to Bob only** (`is_stable_to_bob`). A stable play
  clue to Cathy, which role inversion makes possible, entered no rung. So 4.5, the
  safe stall, took the 3.
- **Resolution (v18.15.0):** 4.1's pool (`pool_stable_play_any_partner`) also
  takes a stable play clue to Cathy under Throw It in a Hole. Rung 3.1 stays
  Bob-only. See reactor0 `DECISION_MAKING.md` §4.1 and tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §2f.
  - Rerun, `logs/yagami_blue-2014538.log --turn 23`: `clue rank value=3 → player 0`
    before, `clue colour value=2 → player 0` (Green to black, 4.1 `stable_play`)
    after.
  - Test:
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2014538_color_stable_play_over_rank_stall.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed in v18.15.0.

### T24 — green locks black with 4 instead of giving Green on the g3

- **Human:** green has a play and black is locked; the 4 gives black no safe action.
  Green knows the g3 is playable and can clue Green to get black to play it.
- **Bot:**
  - Green (1 token, occupied by its n2) gave 4 to black, read as a LOCK
    (`3.bob_chop`, `stable_lock`, rung 3.7).
  - Black was green's Bob and stuck. His chop o25 was a non-critical y4.
  - Blue (green's Cathy) was locked.
  - Green on o26 (the g3, touched by the T23 3) is a stable play reveal.
- **Cause:**
  - Rung 3.1, the stable play clue to Bob, needs 2 clues (1 with Cathy relief),
    and green had 1.
  - Rung 3.7, the lock, has no clue-count condition, so it was the first sub-rung
    that could fire.
  - A lock and a play cost the same one clue. The lock leaves Bob no action; the
    play gives him one.
- **Resolution (v18.16.0):** reactor0 rung **3.6b**. When 3.7 would lock and
  there are fewer clues than 3.1 needs, a stable play clue to Bob takes the lock's
  place, provided his chop is not critical. A critical chop keeps 3.8–3.10. See reactor0 `DECISION_MAKING.md` priority 3, item
  6b, and tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §2g.
  - Rerun, `logs/yagami_green-2014538.log --turn 24`: `clue rank value=4 → player 0`
    before, `clue colour value=2 → player 0` (Green to black, `3.bob_chop`
    `stable_play`) after.
  - Tests:
    - `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2014538_stable_play_over_lock_at_one_clue.cpp`;
    - `tests/test_reactor0/test_decision_making/test_stable_play_before_lock.cpp`: a
      non-critical chop takes the play, and a critical chop keeps the lock.
  - A first version stood on its own ahead of 3.7, so it also clued where no lock
    was coming. In self-play it moved 9 seeds for a net loss of 7 points. As a
    replacement for 3.7's lock only, it moves 6 seeds for a net gain of 14.
- **Status:** fixed in v18.16.0.
