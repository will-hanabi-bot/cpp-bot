## The human's analysis (verbatim)

> Turn 26: When a reactive clue is given, the stacks used is pairwise(giver, reacter).
> yagami and will-bot67 both saw b3 go into the hole on turn 24, so the blue stack is
> at 3. The target for the reactive clue at turn 26 is therefore b4 on slot 1. What I
> suspect may be the issue is that reacter's red stack is not at 3 because will-bot67
> played {y3, r3} on turn 23.
>
> Since this has come up in a number of recent fixes, I would like to clarify as a
> broad, all-encompassing convention for the reactor0 convention in TIIAH called the
> "Actionable Superposition Collapse Rule" (ASCR):
>
> Whenever a player encounters a situation where it seems like they are actioned to
> play an unplayable card (whether they are the reacter or receiver of a clue - and
> this does not even have to be after a clue has resolved), they must first check if
> there are any worlds where that card actually becomes playable before dismissing the
> action. If so, they immediately collapse the worlds to admit only those where the
> card that they were called to play works. In addition, as a receiver, they must
> follow the bucket + finesse suit priority rule - evaluating those suits before
> others. Only if this fails do they move on to the next interpretation, if it exists.
>
> This is an example where the ASCR applies midway through the interpretation of a
> clue. will-bot67 needs to choose a target, and let's say that the pairwise(giver,
> reactor) = b3 stack is resolved and not actually an issue. The first target being
> evaluated here is subject to the ASCR - there exists a world where r3 played, which
> makes r4 playable, so that immediately triggers a superposition collapse here. If
> will-bot67 fails to find a world where r3 actually played, only then do they go on to
> evaluate the next target (g2 on slot 4).
>
> A number of previous versions had fallbacks where before a clue interpretation is
> written off as invalid, it runs ASCR first. I'd like to unify all of these cases so
> that they use the shared code that describes ASCR so that we don't end up with a
> bunch of isolated interpretation bugs.

### T26 — will-bot67 reacts on the g2 instead of the b4, and strikes

- **Human:** the reactive's frame is pairwise(giver, reacter), where blue is on 3, so
  the target is will-bot69's b4 on slot 1. Its pairing calls will-bot67's r4, which
  plays in the world where its own T23 `{y3,r3}` was the r3. ASCR collapses to that
  world. Only if no world worked would the walk go on to the g2 on slot 4.
- **Bot:**
  - T25 (log): black's 1 to will-bot69 is an ordinary reactive, with will-bot67
    reacting.
  - On the frame black and will-bot67 share, `22131`, blue is 3 (both watched the T24
    b3) but red is 2: will-bot67 cannot name o16, its own r3.
  - The walk's first target is the b4 (slot 1). The sum rule pairs it with will-bot67's
    slot 5, o6 `{r4,ra4}`, which cannot play on red 2.
  - So the walk went on to the g2 (slot 4), called o26, and at T26 will-bot67 played it
    into a strike (`precedence.urgent_reaction`, o26).
- **Cause:** the walk (`tiiah::interpret_reactive`) dropped a target whose reacter card
  failed on the frame without trying the worlds. The v19.3.0 world fallback tried them
  only when **no** pairing read at all, and here the g2 pairing did.
- **Resolution (v20.6.0):** the Actionable Superposition Collapse Rule, one shared
  routine (`ascr_find`, `src/conventions/tiiah/superposition.cpp:498-523`; tiiah
  `CONVENTION.md` §1e).
  - The reactive walk now tries each target in the worlds before walking on to the
    next (`ascr_pairing`, `src/conventions/tiiah/interpret_reactive.cpp:550-674`). It
    asks for a strike-free world in which the target plays and the reacter's card
    plays. If one exists, that pairing is the reading, and the worlds collapse to
    those, before the reacter's call is stamped.
  - This subsumes v19.3.0's no-pairing world fallback. v20.5.0's receiver fallback
    reads through the same routine.
  - The stable-call world re-run and "call alive in a world" are not yet migrated;
    they are in `TODO.md` §60.
  - Rerun, `logs/will-bot67-2018541.log --turn 26`: `play(order=26)` (the strike)
    before, `play(order=6)` (the r4) after. o16 settles as the r3 in will-bot67's
    belief and in its row with yagami.
  - Tests:
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_replay_2018541_ascr_reacter_card_plays_in_a_world.cpp`,
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_ascr_walk.cpp`.
