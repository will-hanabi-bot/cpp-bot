### T59 — discard so the seat after next gets two turns

- **Position:** stacks r5 y4 g3 b5 p5 d4 (25), 0 clues, 2 cards left. Every view agrees
  (`scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 9000096 59`: 543554).
  - Still needed: the y5 (sim-bob's o48, called by T58's 3 to sim-cathy, which he
    reacts to), the g4 (sim-cathy's o52, that 3's target), and the g5 (o42) and u5
    (o16, unclued), both sim-alice's.
  - If every seat draws, sim-alice gets one more turn and the game ends at 29.
- **Bot:** sim-bob played the urgent y5 (`precedence.urgent_reaction`).
  - `forced_endgame_action` declined, and the endgame search ran and was not taken.
  - The game went T60 g4, T61 g5, T62 discard, T63 a miss: 29.
- **Cause:**
  - No forced rule discards to win back a token for a stall. Rule 5, "the partner needs
    two turns", needs one card left, a token, and the NEXT seat.
  - A reacter's discard is always read as the reaction (`react_discard`): it would call
    sim-cathy to discard, not keep her owed play.
- **Status:** open (planned for v23.27.0: a forced-endgame stall discard that is not the
  reaction).
