### T28-T33 — green's called o13 is reset when the d2 the team read it as lands elsewhere

- **Human:** black's T28 5 to green is legal: answered by blue's b3 its bucket names
  only the d2 (u2), and green sees that d2 in blue's hand. So green knows o13 is an
  auxiliary playable, the g4 or the b4 once the b3 lands, and plays it.
- **Bot:**
  - T29: blue's b3 (o21) answered; the team read green's o13 `{d2}`, and green, by
    core rule 2 (`own_called_fallback`), `[f] g4,b4`.
  - T30: green's Purple to blue called blue's o8 as the d2; blue played it at T32.
  - T32: the team's `{d2}` emptied (every copy now accounted for). `Game::elim`
    reset the card and cleared the call (`[reset]`); green's own view followed.
  - T33: green discarded o12 (`reactor0.choose_action` rung `11.chuck_leftmost`).
  - `scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2025422 28` and `33`: every view agrees with the truth
    (`023221`, `033322`); this is a reading, not a stack desync.
- **Cause:** `Game::elim` (`src/basics/game.cpp`, the step-2 reset loop) cleared any
  call whose team reading elimination emptied. Core rule 2 (v18.9.0) applied only to
  our own view of our own card, and only while the call stood.
- **Resolution (v23.12.0):** core rule 2 for the team. When elimination empties the
  team's reading of a TIIAH call, the call stands as any playable: what the card can
  still be that is playable on the common stacks and was playable on the shared view
  when it was called (`team_called_fallback`, `ConvData::called_playables`;
  tiiah/CONVENTION.md §1d). o13 stays called as `{g4,b4}` -- not the d3, which became
  playable only when the d2 landed.
  - `build/replay_log.exe logs/yagami_green-2025422.log --turn 33 --rerun`: before
    `discard(order=12)`; after `play(order=13)`.
  - Test: `tests/test_tiiah/test_replay_2025422_team_reading_ruled_out_reads_any_playable.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed in v23.12.0.
