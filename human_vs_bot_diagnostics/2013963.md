- **Cause:** will-bot67's only call was on o17, an **unclued** receiver's call from
  an earlier completed reaction (yagami's T7 play answering will-bot69's T6
  reactive). Both bots held it as CALLED_TO_PLAY, and it was not urgent.
  - v18.3.0 counted only a clued call as a standing play
    (`src/conventions/variants/hole.cpp`, `has_standing_play`). So the reverse
    position didn't hold, and a clue to will-bot67 read as stable.
- **Resolution (v18.10.0):**
  - **Rule:** a settled (not urgent) call counts as a standing play too; a pending
    reaction call still doesn't.
  - **A fix takes precedence over a reverse reactive** (the reviewer's rule, given
    with this report): Cathy played a card matching one of the candidates of Bob's
    called card, so Bob's call is in superposition with a dead card from her view.
    Alice's clue then identifies the called card as that dead card, so both Bob and
    Cathy know it is a fix. That keeps 2013726 T5, where the call fixed is a settled
    receiver's call, read as a fix at every seat, black's included. In 2013963 T10
    the giver and the reacter both see the standing call is a still-good g1, so it
    is the reverse reactive. Test: `tests/test_tiiah/test_fix_before_reverse_reactive.cpp`.
  - **Reruns:**
