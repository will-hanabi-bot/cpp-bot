### T14-T16 — a reverse reactive Yellow is ignored

- **Bot:** sim-bob's Yellow to sim-cathy (o19, o17); sim-cathy plays o19, her known
  y1, at T15, and sim-alice answers with her slot 5 (o0) at T16. The call lands on
  sim-cathy's o17, the y2 (the "yellow 2" of the analysis), and at T18 she clues Red
  to sim-alice instead of playing it.
- **Why:** the same as [9000174](9000174.md) T14. The reverse reactive's frame left
  out sim-cathy's own y1, whose identity her seat cannot see, so at her seat the call
  read o17 as the y1, already down, and Rule 5 dropped it as stale. Every other seat
  read `{y2}`.
- **Fix:** the receiver's own called cards enter the frame as their team reading
  (`stacks_after_queued_plays`, tiiah/CONVENTION.md §1d).
- **Replay:** `replay_log logs/diag/bk/sim-cathy-9000126.log --turn 18 --rerun`:
  v23.14.0 `discard(order=13)`; v23.15.0 `play(order=17)`.
- **Test:** `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_selfplay_9000126_reverse_reactive_receiver_plays_y2.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed in v23.15.0
